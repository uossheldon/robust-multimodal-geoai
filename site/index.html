Two sensors. Four algae-severity classes. A frozen comparison across two September dates.

01

Study design

Two sensors. One geographic grid.

Sentinel-2 optical RGB and Sentinel-1 VV/VH radar are aligned to the same 224×224 tiles over Lough Neagh. DeepLab baselines test optical, SAR and early fusion; modality dropout and simulated optical occlusion test resilience. Frozen TerraMind provides a foundation-model comparison.

Methods and fixed split

DeepLabV3-MobileNetV3-Large uses RGB, VV/VH, or five-channel early fusion. All selected DeepLab models use the same class-weighted loss. Controlled training keeps 10 epochs, batch size 8, optimizer policy and spatial augmentations fixed. Modality dropout keeps both sensors for 50% of samples and removes each sensor for 25%; occlusion training additionally corrupts half of intact samples, with fractions sampled from 10–70%.

Frozen terramind_v1_tiny uses RGB + S1RTC and a lightweight four-class decoder, trained for 10 epochs at batch size 4. Rasterio uses the B04 grid, bilinear imagery resampling and nearest-neighbour labels; each modality is normalized separately.

TRAIN: 2025-01-31, 03-12, 04-08, 04-09, 05-16, 05-18, 05-21, 08-12. Validation: 2025-01-01 and 2025-06-20. TEST: 2025-09-08 and 2025-09-21.

02

Key findings

Resilience is only part of the problem.

01 / Resilience

Robust training helps under optical degradation.

Occlusion-aware training increased resilience to severe simulated optical degradation relative to modality dropout alone.

02 / Task matters

Binary detection and severity mapping differ.

Frozen TerraMind produced strong binary algae detection, without consistently stronger four-class temporal generalisation.

03 / Time matters

Every model lost ground in September.

All evaluated models lost four-class performance from validation to the held-out dates, highlighting temporal/domain shift.

05

Optical degradation & missing sensors

Training for missing information.

Occlusion-trained fusion retains numerically higher Macro mIoU at every measured optical-occlusion level. Complete loss of Sentinel-1 remains difficult for both robust methods.

06

The remaining challenge

Every model lost ground over time.

Four-class performance declined on the September dates for all six methods. The result is consistent with temporal/domain shift within one region; it does not establish geographic generalisation or its physical cause.

07

Foundation models & uncertainty

Different strengths. Persistent limits.

Frozen TerraMind

A frozen RGB + S1RTC backbone with a lightweight decoder achieved strong validation segmentation and binary test detection. Its four-class test performance did not consistently exceed the conventional models. TerraMind robustness was not evaluated.

Ensemble uncertainty

Probability ensembling improved validation calibration, but error-detection AUROC remained modest and missing-modality behaviour inconsistent. Better calibration did not establish reliable failure detection.

08

Interpreting the evidence

Evaluation notes & limitations

This is a small regional study with two September TEST dates, severe class imbalance and limited mid-algae segmentation. Temporal separation does not establish geographic generalisation. Uncertainty is diagnostic, not a reliable safety mechanism.

Evaluation notes
  • Clean multi-seed and missing-modality results use sample SD across training seeds 42, 7 and 123. Conventional baselines are single runs without a seed SD estimate.
  • Optical-occlusion TEST summaries pool 3 training seeds × corruption seeds 101, 202 and 303: 9 runs. Their SDs describe a different population from clean/missing results; neither is a confidence interval. Validation robustness summaries average corruption within each training seed before calculating SD.
  • Sentinel-2 evaluates 3,474,284 valid pixels; SAR/fusion/TerraMind evaluate 3,169,093 because invalid SAR targets are ignored. The comparison is not perfectly pixel-matched.
  • Simulated optical occlusion is not measured real cloud cover. Missing/occluded normalized channels are zeroed. Non-monotonic scores do not imply that removing optical information improves imagery.
  • Earlier Phase 2D unweighted Sentinel-2 TEST access exists and is preserved. Phase 7 followed frozen development and validation selection; no post-Phase-7 tuning or checkpoint selection occurred. It was not the first historical TEST access.
  • No statistical-significance test establishes model superiority. Binary algae detection is distinct from four-class severity mapping.
  • Completed checkpoint metadata supports the three-seed TerraMind validation summary; older partial exports are historical. Phase 5B single-model entropy was scored against ensemble correctness, limiting direct failure-detection comparisons.
