01

THE SETUP

Two sensors. One geographic grid.

Optical and radar offer different observations. Their combination still has to survive missing information.

A regional Lough Neagh dataset covers 12 acquisition dates in 2025. Rasterio aligns Sentinel-2 B04/B03/B02 and Sentinel-1 VV/VH GAMMA0_TERRAIN dB to B04. Imagery uses bilinear resampling; masks use nearest-neighbour only. The fixed manifest reads 224×224 tiles on demand.

02

CONTROLLED PROGRESSION

Fusion is a starting point.
Robustness must be tested.

BASELINES

Optical, SAR & early fusion

DeepLabV3-MobileNetV3-Large takes RGB, VV/VH, or five concatenated channels. RGB reflectance and SAR are normalized separately. Validation selects the weighted S2 baseline and all final checkpoints.

DIAGNOSIS / VALIDATION

Naive fusion is brittle

0.2425 → 0.0133

Clean to 70% simulated optical occlusion, macro mIoU for one frozen fusion model. The corruption score averages three fixed mask seeds; this is validation evidence, not TEST.

INTERVENTION 01

Modality dropout

Keep both sensors for 50% of training samples, zero S1 for 25%, or zero S2 for 25%. Missing information is represented by zeros after normalization; both modalities are never dropped together.

INTERVENTION 02

Occlusion-aware training

Keep the dropout policy. Occlude half of intact both-modality samples, with a fraction uniformly sampled from 10–70%. Coherent irregular masks zero normalized RGB only. These are not real clouds.

Controlled DeepLab experiments: same architecture, split, 10 epochs, batch size 8, augmentations, optimizer policy and class-weighted loss. Training seeds: 42, 7, 123. Corruption seeds: 101, 202, 303.

04

OPTICAL DEGRADATION & MISSING SENSORS

Robustness, with qualifications.

The observation

At 70% occlusion, dropout scores 0.1630 ± 0.0055 mIoU versus 0.2122 ± 0.0115 for occlusion-trained fusion. Missing S1 gives 0.1141 ± 0.0166 / 0.1204 ± 0.0264; missing S2 gives 0.1690 ± 0.0176 / 0.1993 ± 0.0164, respectively.

The interpretation

The non-monotonic curve does not mean hiding imagery adds information. Corruption-aware training changes prediction behaviour and class balance under the September distribution. These controlled perturbations do not establish real-cloud performance or physical causation.

05

FOUNDATION-MODEL BENCHMARK

Frozen TerraMind.
A different trade-off.

Strong validation segmentation and binary TEST discrimination, without consistent four-class superiority.

terramind_v1_tiny receives RGB + S1RTC. The pretrained backbone stays frozen; a lightweight decoder turns its final 14×14 feature map into four-class 224×224 logits.

Three-seed validation mIoU: 0.2771 ± 0.0138. TEST mIoU: 0.1718 ± 0.0177. TEST binary algae Dice: 0.6821 ± 0.0025. Completed checkpoint metadata supports the validation summary; older partial exports remain historical. This is not full fine-tuning, and TerraMind robustness was not evaluated.

All conventional baselines also declined: S2 0.2274 → 0.1698, S1 0.2349 → 0.1756, and naive fusion 0.2425 → 0.1658 (single runs). These drops are consistent with substantial temporal/domain shift.

TerraMind's per-date mIoU was 0.0965 ± 0.0433 on September 8 and 0.2335 ± 0.0100 on September 21. Two dates in one region are insufficient to establish broad generalisation. These observations prompted no post-TEST changes.

07

SECONDARY VALIDATION ANALYSIS

Better calibration is not reliable failure detection.

For the occlusion-trained models, probability ensembling reduced clean validation ECE from 0.2019 to 0.1757, NLL from 1.1407 to 1.0680 and Brier from 0.6688 to 0.6436, compared with mean individual-model diagnostics.

Disagreement AUROC was only 0.5984 at 70% occlusion and 0.3897 with S1 missing. Missing-modality behaviour was inconsistent. Softmax entropy is not automatically calibrated uncertainty; the project does not claim reliable failure detection.

Uncertainty comparison scope

Phase 5 uses validation data and corruption seed 101, without calibration fitting. The ensemble comparison's single-model entropy score is measured against ensemble correctness, which limits direct single-model versus ensemble error-detection claims. The full audit preserves that distinction.

08

BOUNDARIES OF THE EVIDENCE

What this study does not establish.

  • A small regional dataset and only two September TEST dates limit temporal and geographic claims.
  • Severe class imbalance, particularly high algae, and low mid-class IoU limit severity mapping.
  • Synthetic optical occlusion is not measured real cloud cover, haze, shadows or cloud-mask error.
  • Earlier Phase 2D TEST access and unequal valid-pixel support qualify the final comparison.
  • Three-seed variation is not statistical significance; frozen TerraMind is not full fine-tuning.
  • Uncertainty error detection remains modest and inconsistent. Data/label provenance and derived-figure rights remain unresolved.
