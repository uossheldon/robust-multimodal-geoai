## Preprocessing Summary

Day 1 Part 2:

- Uses RGB only in `B04`, `B03`, `B02` order.
- Confirms the three RGB bands share width, height, CRS and transform.
- Warps masks to the B04 grid using nearest-neighbour `WarpedVRT`.
- Extracts non-overlapping `224 x 224` tiles with stride `224`.
- Ignores optical nodata and non-finite pixels by setting mask pixels to `255`.
- Keeps tiles with at least 20% valid labelled pixels.
- Saves `rgb.npy`, `masks.npy`, and `manifest.csv` under split folders.
- Loads masks by merging classes `1`, `2`, and `3` into algae class `1`; background remains `0`; `255` is ignored.
- Scales RGB DN by `10000` to reflectance for DeepLab, then applies ImageNet normalization.
- Converts RGB reflectance to BGR `[0, 255]` for TerraMind.

Day 2:

- Uses RGB in `B04`, `B03`, `B02` order and SAR in `VV`, `VH` order.
- Uses B04 as the reference grid.
- Warps masks with nearest-neighbour resampling.
- Warps VV/VH to B04 using bilinear resampling.
- Validates SAR metadata: `output_scale = "db"`, `back_coefficient = "GAMMA0_TERRAIN"`, `nodata = -9999`.
- Builds fixed selected subsets: 64 train, 16 validation, 16 test tiles.
- Requires common valid pixels across optical, SAR, and mask.
- Saves `tiles_sar/<split>/sar.npy`, `masks.npy`, `manifest.csv`.
- Saves `tiles_fused/<split>/fused.npy`, `rgb.npy`, `masks.npy`, `manifest.csv`.
- Fits SAR mean/std from valid training pixels only; fills SAR nodata with the training mean before saving.
- Keeps fused channel order `R`, `G`, `B`, `VV`, `VH`.

## Reconstructability

Likely reconstructable from public Sentinel sources:

- Sentinel-2 optical bands `B02`, `B03`, `B04`, `B08` for the listed dates and Lough Neagh coverage, subject to exact product discovery, cloud conditions, processing level, CRS/grid, and crop bounds.
- Sentinel-1 VV/VH backscatter for nearby acquisition times, subject to orbit, acquisition mode, preprocessing recipe, terrain correction, dB scaling, and crop bounds.
- Derived `NDVI` and `NDWI`, if the exact formulas, scaling, nodata policy, and source bands are known.

Not independently reconstructable from public Sentinel sources without extra provenance:

- The `masks/*.tiff` segmentation labels.
- Exact archive crop extents, tile eligibility, manually prepared masks, and any instructor curation.
- Any selected tile manifests and trained checkpoints.
